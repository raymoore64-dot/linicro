Linicro Support

Linicro is ten retro arcade games on an invented early-2000s keypad phone, for iPhone. Beat daily dares, collect phones, raise a pocket pet and hunt for secret codes.

Contact

For help, bug reports or feedback, email DialInnStudio26@protonmail.com. We usually reply within a few days. If something went wrong, telling us your iPhone model and iOS version helps.

How to play

ControlsTap the on-screen D-pad or keypad. OK (or 5) selects, the red C key goes back, and 2 4 6 8 also move. # pauses a game.

PowerHold the red C key to switch the phone off, and hold it again to boot it back up.

Menu shortcuts7 opens your messages, 8 settings, 0 sound and 9 music. The right soft key opens the phone picker.

Common questions

How do I unlock more games?Open WAP Downloads from the menu. Signal Hopper unlocks at 2,500 lifetime points and Dial-Up Rally at 6,000. Buzz Fishing unlocks after you beat two daily dares, and Ring Rhythm needs a secret word (your in-game mate Kaz drops hints).

How do I unlock more phones?Every point you score counts towards your lifetime total. The SOLARA, MINTRA, AURUM and VOLTA phones unlock at 500, 1,500, 3,500 and 7,000 points. One prototype phone is hidden behind a secret code.

Who is texting me?Mum, Dex, Kaz and the others are fictional characters in the game. Nothing is sent to or from real people, and the "WAP downloads" are part of the game too. No data is downloaded.

When does the Daily Challenge change?A new challenge starts at midnight on your phone's local date. The Game Center Daily Challenge leaderboard rolls over at 00:00 UTC.

Do I need Game Center?No. It's optional. If you're signed in to Game Center on your iPhone (Settings > Game Center), your best scores go to 12 leaderboards and you can earn 15 achievements. The game is fully playable without it.

Do I need an account or internet connection?No. Linicro has no accounts and plays fully offline. Game Center uses the internet only if you're signed in.

Where is my progress saved?Scores, unlocks, your pet and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?No.

Can I turn off sound, music or vibration?Yes. Use 0 for sound and 9 for music on the menu, or open settings with 8.

Which devices are supported?iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

The phone brands in Linicro are invented. Linicro is not affiliated with any phone manufacturer.
