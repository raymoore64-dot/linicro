Privacy Policy: Linicro

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Linicro, made by Studio Linicro, does not collect any personal information. It doesn't store any on a server and doesn't send it anywhere.

  • The app has no accounts, sign-in or profiles of its own.
  • Your progress (best scores, lifetime points, unlocked phones and games, your Pocket Pet and its name, daily streaks, found codes) and your settings are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • The app itself makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • The text messages in the game come from fictional characters. Nothing is sent to or received from real people.
  • The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game sends your scores and achievements to Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. Apple handles this under Apple's Privacy Policy. Studio Linicro doesn't receive your Game Center identity or any other personal data. You can play without Game Center.

Sharing

If you choose to share a score card, Linicro opens the standard iOS share sheet with an image and short text. Where it goes is up to you. Linicro sends nothing by itself.

Children

Linicro collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future update ever changes any of this, this page will be updated first and the change explained here in plain language.
